# Equipment

- **Page:** `/equipement` is an encyclopedia of equippable items: search, type, "wearable at my level", official effects.
- **Data:** 21,622 items and 941 sets from dofusdude `3.7.14.18` (same version as the game data).
- **Slots used by builds** (by item super type): weapon, shield, hat, cloak, amulet, ring, belt, boots, pet/mount, Dofus and trophies.
- **Not shown:** rarity (none in the data), prices (no reliable source).
