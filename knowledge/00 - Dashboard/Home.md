# Atlas des Douze

A free companion site for DOFUS (Unity, game version **3.7.14.18**). It tracks quests, achievements and Dofus, shows builds and professions, and computes everything from the official game files. Fan site, not affiliated with Ankama.

> **Now:** pre-launch. The in-site world map was removed on 2026-11-20 (external links instead), and a full security audit was done. Waiting on launch decisions. See [[Current Status]].
