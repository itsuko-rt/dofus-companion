# Pandawa (breed id 12)

**Role in the site:** one of the two focus classes. Class colour: ochre.

## From the game files (VERIFIED, 3.7.14.18)
- 44 spells (22 slots × 2 variants). Full effects at `/personnage/sorts/12`.
- **States** (checked in the data, `docs/RAPPORT_ARCHITECTURE.md` §6):
  - Karcham (level 10) and Chamrak (level 120) are two variants of one slot, and both apply the **Porteur** state;
  - **Saoul** is applied by 9 spells: Ribote, Bombance, Picole, Alcoshu, Liqueur, Ébriété, Ivresse, Absinthe, Bistouille;
  - **Sobre** is applied by 5: Gueule de Bois, Bombance, Picole, Lait de Bambou, Prohibition;
  - the Tonneau appears in Ébriété and Ivresse.
- The data says *which* spells set which states, not *why* or *when* to play them.
